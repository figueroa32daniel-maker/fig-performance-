Who we train

Most clients are 40 to 70, work demanding jobs, and already "eat pretty healthy." They want to lose fat, build muscle and feel strong again, and they're tired of guessing. Many have hurt a back, knee or shoulder before, so every plan starts around what your body can do today.

How personal training works

  1. Free consult and InBody scan. About an hour: body fat, muscle and visceral fat, a strength assessment and your goals.
  2. Your plan. Training two or more times a week, plus a simple nutrition plan built from foods you already like.
  3. Train one-on-one. 50-minute sessions with Coach Dan. We track every lift so you get stronger each week.
  4. Recover. Red light therapy and vibration therapy are part of the routine.
  5. Re-scan. InBody shows what changed, then we adjust.

Real results from Placentia clients
