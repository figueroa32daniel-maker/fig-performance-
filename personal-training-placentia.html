Here's exactly what happens when you train one-on-one at Fig Performance, a private personal training gym in Placentia: a free consult with an InBody scan, a plan built around your body, 50-minute sessions with Coach Dan Figueroa (ACSM certified), recovery, and a re-scan to prove it worked. No crowds, no waiting for equipment, no guessing.

Who we train

Most clients are 40 to 70, work demanding jobs, and already "eat pretty healthy." They want to lose fat, build muscle and feel strong again, and they're tired of guessing. Many have hurt a back, knee or shoulder before, so every plan starts around what your body can do today.

How personal training works

  1. Free consult and InBody scan. About an hour: body fat, muscle and visceral fat, a strength assessment and your goals.
  2. Your plan. Training two or more times a week, plus a simple nutrition plan built from foods you already like.
  3. Train one-on-one. 50-minute sessions with Coach Dan. We track every lift so you get stronger each week.
  4. Recover. Red light therapy and vibration therapy are part of the routine.
  5. Re-scan. InBody shows what changed, then we adjust.

Real results from Placentia clients

  • Sam, 70: deadlifts about 135 pounds for reps, dumbbell bench presses the 50s and presses 40-pound dumbbells overhead.
  • One client lost 10 pounds in her first four weeks.

FAQ

Straight answers.

How much does a personal trainer cost in Placentia?

At Fig Performance, personal training is $90 a session with Coach Dan, billed monthly, with a 3-month minimum. You save 20% when you pay 3 months in full. The first consult and InBody scan are free.

What happens in the first session?

The first visit is the free consult: an InBody scan, a movement and strength assessment and a conversation about your goals. Your first training session starts from what that showed, usually with the basic strength patterns at a weight you can control.

How often should I train with a personal trainer?

Many Fig Performance clients over 40 train twice a week in 50-minute sessions and add daily walking. Some train three or four times a week.

What areas do you serve?

Fig Performance is at 1284 E. Yorba Linda Blvd in Placentia and serves Placentia, Yorba Linda, Brea, Fullerton, Anaheim Hills and Villa Park. Concierge training at your home or office is also available.

Do I need to be in shape to start?

No. Plans start where your body is today. If you're under a doctor's care for an injury, check with your doctor first.
