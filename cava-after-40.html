Stop ordering the curated bowls at CAVA. Each one is close to a day of salt.

Build it yourself and get the same protein for about half the sodium.

CAVA's curated bowls, the pre-designed ones on the board with names, run somewhere between about 1,670 and 2,210 mg of sodium each. The daily value is 2,300. So the healthy-looking Mediterranean bowl you ordered because it was not a burger is most of a day's salt in one sitting. Build the same bowl yourself and you can get the same protein for roughly half of that.

The curated bowls are the trap

This is a restaurant where the pre-built option is worse than the one you assemble, and that is unusual. Curated bowls land roughly between 580 and 860 calories, and every single one of them sits in that 1,670 to 2,210 mg sodium band.

A bowl you build yourself, greens base with grilled chicken, hummus, tzatziki and a light dressing, comes to about 395 calories with roughly 37 grams of protein and around 1,045 mg of sodium. Same restaurant, same ingredients, half the salt.

The grilled chicken is the anchor

Six independent nutrition sources agree on this one, which is rare:

  • Grilled Chicken: 250 calories, 28 g protein, 13 g fat, 3 g carbs, 670 mg sodium
  • Grilled Steak: about 170 calories, roughly 23 g protein
  • Power Greens base: about 30 calories, 2 g protein, 35 mg sodium

A greens base costs you almost nothing in calories or salt, which is exactly what you want it to do. It leaves the whole budget for protein.

The counterintuitive one: steak is lower sodium than chicken

CAVA's grilled steak carries roughly 390 mg less sodium than its grilled chicken. Nobody expects that. A SuperGreens bowl with grilled steak and tzatziki lands near 235 calories with about 28 grams of protein and only around 375 mg of sodium.

If blood pressure is the thing you are managing rather than calories, steak is the better order here, and that sentence is true at almost no other restaurant in these guides.

The pre-made bowl is the problem. At most places it is the opposite.

Coach Dan Figueroa

Falafel is not a protein

A serving of falafel is about 350 calories with roughly 6 grams of protein and around 810 mg of sodium. It is chickpeas, but it is fried chickpeas, and six grams is not a meal's worth of anything.

If you are eating vegetarian here, the falafel is the item that feels like the protein and is not. Build around the dips and the greens instead, and know that you are eating a carbohydrate dish.

What I would order

  1. Build your own, never curated. Greens base, grilled protein, two dips, light dressing. Roughly 395 calories and 37 g of protein.
  2. Double grilled chicken if you want the protein high. That lands near 500 calories for about 56 g, though it also pushes sodium toward 1,340 mg.
  3. Grilled steak instead of chicken if sodium is your priority. About 390 mg less.
  4. Greens base, not grains, unless you have earned the carbs. The base is where the calories hide.

The 15-second version

If you remember one thing

  • Grilled chicken: 250 cal and 28 g protein

Why this one is on the list

Because CAVA is where people go when they have decided to be good, the same way they go to Panera. And like Panera, the place is fine and the default order is not. The difference is that here the fix is completely free: walk past the board with the named bowls and build the thing yourself.

Thirty-seven grams of protein for under 400 calories is a very good lunch. You just have to not order the one with the name on it.

A note on these numbers

Six independent nutrition breakdowns give CAVA's grilled chicken identically at 250 calories, 28 grams of protein and 670 mg of sodium, which is unusually strong agreement. One source claims 33 grams of protein for the same item; six against one, so this page uses 28. Built-bowl figures depend entirely on what goes in the bowl, so the numbers above describe the specific builds named rather than any bowl you make. Portions vary with whoever is serving. Check CAVA's own nutrition calculator if you are tracking closely.

Same idea every time: find the protein, spot the one item doing the damage, and order so you are not eating differently from everyone else at the table.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is CAVA healthy?

It can be, but the default is not. CAVA's curated bowls run between roughly 1,670 and 2,210 mg of sodium each, against a 2,300 mg daily value. A bowl you build yourself off a greens base with grilled chicken and two dips is about 395 calories with roughly 37 grams of protein and around 1,045 mg of sodium.

How much protein is in CAVA grilled chicken?

28 grams for 250 calories, with 13 g of fat and 670 mg of sodium. Six independent nutrition sources agree on that figure, which makes it one of the more reliable numbers in these guides.

Should I order a curated bowl or build my own at CAVA?

Build your own. The curated bowls are the pre-designed ones on the menu board, and every one of them sits in the 1,670 to 2,210 mg sodium range. Building the same ingredients yourself gets similar protein for roughly half the salt.

Is falafel a good protein at CAVA?

No. A serving is about 350 calories with roughly 6 grams of protein and around 810 mg of sodium. It is fried chickpeas. It feels like the protein in a vegetarian order and it is not.

What is the lowest sodium order at CAVA?

A SuperGreens base with grilled steak and tzatziki, which lands near 235 calories with about 28 grams of protein and only around 375 mg of sodium. CAVA's steak carries roughly 390 mg less sodium than its chicken, which surprises most people.
