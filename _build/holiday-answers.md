# Holiday post: Dan's answers (Nov 3, 2026, voice memo)

- Thanksgiving advice: eat as much protein as you can first, then vegetables, fiber and other nutrient-dense foods, so you feel full and have less room for the stuff that doesn't support your goals. It's the holidays and we bond over food with family and friends, so live a little. But if you have goals, act in accordance with them.
- Dan's own holiday: he still trains on Thanksgiving Day ("you got to have that balance"). He trains Christmas morning if he has time, but family time comes first. At family dinner he stacks protein and vegetables, adds a little carbs, and leaves room for dessert because he likes dessert. He eats 80/20: 80% clean, nutrient-dense food and 20% foods he enjoys. "In order for your nutrition to be sustainable, it has to be flexible and it has to be enjoyable."
- Clients who did well: protein first, and most of the plate protein with the rest vegetables. They skipped the carbs to save room for a piece of pie or another holiday dessert.
- Biggest mistake over 40: drinking way too much, every weekend before and after Thanksgiving. Also dragging out holiday food for weeks. Thanksgiving and Christmas are a couple of days, maybe a week each, then you move on, not all of November and December. Set boundaries, have self-control, say no, and don't give in to peer pressure ("come on, live a little, it's the holidays").
- Travel: he gives clients a hotel program. Get as much movement as you can, prioritize protein and fiber, and bring your supplements. No formal check-ins; clients message him if they need support.
- Why start now: habits take time. If you learn to do it when it's hard, it's easier when it's easy. November and December are the hardest months: cold, rain, busy, stressed, shopping, traveling. Learn discipline, self-control and boundaries now, and January through summer gets easier.
