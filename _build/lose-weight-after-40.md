# Post: How to lose weight after 40 — Dan's interview answers

Target: "how to lose weight after 40" (880/mo, KD 15) + "best way to lose weight after 40" (260) + "how to lose weight after 40 female" (110). Link from /why-am-i-not-losing-weight and /fitness-after-40.

## Q1. First thing you look at
Lifestyle routine. Sedentary job? Enough protein? Moving enough? Actually getting stronger in workouts (tracking strength)? Strength -> muscle mass (byproduct) -> higher BMR, more calories burned at rest. Corporate/sedentary job: bottleneck is movement. Typically 2,000-5,000 steps/day; need at least 7,000-8,000 (Dan said "7 to 8" — confirm thousand). Raises NEAT; with both up, "metabolism is fired up."

## Q2. Biggest mistake
Not eating enough protein, and not lifting heavy enough to create mechanical tension / stimulate muscle growth. Build strength over time -> build muscle.

## Q3. Food
Nutrition questionnaire (proteins, carbs, fats, foods they enjoy). Take what they already eat, add structure and intention: raise protein, set calories, create the deficit. Plan with rotating options (breakfast A/B, lunch A/B, snack A/B...). Tracking is fine if they want, but the point is: follow the plan and you'll see results.

## Q4. Training frequency
Many clients train 2x/week. 50-minute session: warm-up, workout, cool-down. Efficient; build strength every week.

## Q5. Strength vs cardio
Both. Everyone needs strength training with age: bone density and muscle mass decline, hormones fluctuate; muscles support bones. Sedentary jobs, less movement, more tired. Leg strength predicts longevity (falls, getting up from a chair/floor). Also train VO2 max (cardio).
