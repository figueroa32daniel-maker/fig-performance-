Meal Plan for Weight Loss

By Coach Dan Figueroa · ACSM CPT · Precision Nutrition Level 1
The one rule: every meal and snack has protein, carbs, fats and fiber. It keeps you full, fuels your brain and workouts, and helps you keep muscle while you lose fat.

Build your plate

  • A palm of protein (lean, most of the time)
  • A fist of vegetables
  • High-fiber carbs fill the rest
  • Don't cut carbs to zero: adults need about 130 g a day, more if you're active
  • Protein: 0.7–1 g per lb of body weight, or at least your goal weight in grams

Keep it 80/20

  • About 80% whole, nutrient-dense food
  • About 20% food you simply enjoy
  • Flexible + enjoyable = sustainable
  • Swap in foods you like, as long as each meal still has all four

Breakfast

  • Quick: protein shake (30+ g protein) + sourdough toast with sugar-free jelly, avocado or peanut butter. Optional: 1 tbsp peanut butter in the shake
  • Yogurt bowl: nonfat Greek yogurt, blueberries or raspberries, a little granola, a drizzle of honey
  • Bigger: ½ cup egg whites + 1 egg, or turkey bacon, turkey or chicken sausage, with sourdough toast or a low-carb bagel

Lunch & dinner

  • Protein + carb: chicken, turkey, lean steak or salmon with sweet potato, rice, whole wheat pasta, quinoa or bulgur, plus vegetables
  • Salad bowl: chicken, lots of vegetables, a carb, light dressing (e.g. Bolthouse Farms)
  • Sandwich: sourdough, turkey or ham (~30 g protein), vegetables, mustard
  • Dinner: same template. Rotate proteins and carbs

Snacks

  • Protein bar + berries
  • Protein shake + a few almonds + fruit
  • Greek yogurt cup
  • Beef jerky or protein chips + whole wheat crackers
  • Always: protein + fiber

Sample day

  • Breakfast: protein shake + toast with peanut butter
  • Lunch: chicken, sweet potato, vegetables
  • Snack: Greek yogurt cup + berries
  • Dinner: salmon, brown rice, vegetables
  • Snack: protein bar + an apple

Grocery list

  • Protein: chicken breast, turkey, lean steak, salmon/fish, eggs & egg whites, turkey bacon, chicken/turkey sausage, nonfat Greek yogurt, protein powder, protein bars, beef jerky
  • Carbs: sourdough, low-carb bagels, sweet potatoes, brown/white rice, whole wheat pasta, quinoa, bulgur, granola, whole wheat crackers
  • Fruit: blueberries, raspberries, blackberries, apples, bananas
  • Vegetables: salad greens + any vegetables you'll actually eat
  • Fats: avocado, peanut butter, almonds, olive oil
  • Extras: sugar-free jelly, honey, mustard, light dressing

Eating out & drinks

  • Splurging is fine, but if your goal has a deadline, every splurge pushes it back a little
  • Chasing the same goal for a long time? Set personal boundaries
  • Do weekday drinks plus brunch, girls' night, guys' night, game day or family events fit your goals? Is there room to cut back?
  • A glass of wine can be a treat if it fits your plan, but it isn't a health food
