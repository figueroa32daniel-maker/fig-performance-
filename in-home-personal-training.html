In-Home Personal Training in North Orange County

Dan comes to your garage, spare room or patio gym, brings extra accessories, and coaches you one-on-one on the equipment you already own.

Who it's for

For adults over 40 with a home gym

You've built a garage gym or a spare-room rack and want a coach running every set. Maybe your calendar is packed, or you'd rather not lift in front of strangers. Think of it as an at-home personal trainer who already knows your goals and your gear.

Goals we coach

Strength, mobility, body composition

Most programs focus on one or more of these.

  • Getting stronger

    Strength work sits at the center of every program. Loads build gradually, at a pace that fits your experience and how well you recover between sessions.

  • Moving more comfortably

    Mobility drills and controlled lifting aim to make everyday tasks, like climbing stairs, carrying groceries or getting down to the floor, feel easier.

  • Body composition

    If changing how much muscle and fat you carry is the goal, training pairs with practical nutrition guidance from a Precision Nutrition Level 1 certified coach.

What changes when Dan comes to you

  • No drive, no parking, no waiting for equipment

    Your session starts when you walk into your own gym.

  • Privacy behind your own door

    Train without an audience.

  • Consistency gets easier

    Training at home removes the drive, so it's easier to keep the session on the calendar.

  • A plan written for your gear

    Nothing calls for a machine you don't own.

  • One client per session

    Dan's attention stays on your form, your breathing and your progress from warm-up to cooldown.

Getting started

Three steps to your first session

  1. 1

    Free phone call

    A conversation about your goals, your training history and what's in your home gym.

  2. 2

    Dan visits, looks over your setup and checks how you move, so the program starts at the right level.

  3. 3

    Sessions begin on a schedule you choose, and the program progresses as you get stronger.

In-home training questions

Anything else on your mind? Call or text (714) 396-7178.

Can a personal trainer come to your house?

Yes. Through in-home personal training, Coach Dan comes to your home gym for private one-on-one sessions and brings gym accessories with him. We serve Placentia, Yorba Linda, Brea, La Habra, Fullerton, Anaheim, Orange, Villa Park, Chino Hills, Diamond Bar, Whittier, La Mirada, Rowland Heights and nearby areas. To get started, book a free call.

Do I need a home gym?

Yes. This service is designed for people who already have a home gym. No home gym? Train with Dan at the Placentia studio instead.

What equipment do you bring?

Dan brings gym accessories to supplement what you own. Describe your setup on the free call, and the exact items are planned around it.

How much space do I need?

Enough room to move freely around your equipment. A garage, spare room or covered patio can work, and Dan confirms the fit during the assessment at your home.

How far will an in home personal trainer travel?

Dan travels to home gyms across North Orange County and a few neighboring areas, from Placentia and Yorba Linda out to Whittier, La Mirada, Chino Hills, Diamond Bar and Rowland Heights. The full list of 13 cities appears above. Close to the edge of that map? Call or text (714) 396-7178 to confirm before you book.

When can sessions be scheduled?

Times are set around your calendar and Dan's travel between homes, so open windows depend on your city and the days you prefer. Share your ideal days and times on the free call, and we'll match you with what's available. Keeping the same time each week tends to make the habit easier to stick with.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel a session?

Tell Dan as early as you can. Rescheduling and cancellation terms are spelled out in your written training agreement, and we'll go over them on your call before you commit to anything. That way you know exactly how changes work from the very first week.

What happens on the first call?

It's a free, relaxed conversation. We talk through your goals, any aches or injuries to work around, your schedule and what you train with now. If it sounds like a good match, we set up the in-home assessment.

Can you train me around an injury?

Share any injuries, aches or past surgeries on your call so they shape the plan from the start. Dan adjusts exercise choices, range of motion and loading around what you tell him. If you're under a doctor's care or recovering from an injury, check with your doctor before starting a new program. Coaching is not a substitute for medical treatment or physical therapy.

How should I prepare for a session at home?

Wear comfortable clothes you can move in and supportive athletic shoes, and keep a water bottle within reach. Clear the floor around your equipment so there's room to move safely, and have a towel handy. If anything has changed with how you feel since the last workout, mention it when Dan arrives so the day's plan can be adjusted.

Can I also train at the Placentia studio?

Yes. Fig Performance also offers individual coaching at our studio, 1284 E. Yorba Linda Blvd in Placentia. Let Dan know on your call which setting you prefer.

Is it fully private?

Yes. Every session is one-on-one in your home: no other clients, no classes, no audience.

Can I use insurance, HSA or FSA funds for training?

Fig Performance does not bill health insurance. You may be able to use HSA or FSA funds with a letter of medical necessity from your own doctor, and you can ask us for a receipt to submit to your plan, though reimbursement isn't guaranteed. It's also worth asking your employer about wellness or fitness stipends. Confirm the details with your plan administrator first.
