The consultation

What actually happens when you come in.

Forty-five minutes, it costs nothing, and there is no pitch waiting at the end of it. Here is the whole thing, so you know before you book.

1

You get met at the door

Your coach greets you and walks you around the room before anything else happens. The machines, the equipment, where everything lives. You see the place first.

2

We sit down and talk about what you actually want

At the consultation booth, in your words. The outcome you are after, the body you want, the way you want to feel. Your goals with training and with food. Then the two that matter most: what is stopping you from getting there on your own, and what is holding you back.

3

The InBody scan

Muscle mass, body fat, weight, and how balanced you are side to side. Those numbers also give us the markers we use to estimate how long your goal should realistically take, so the timeline is not a guess.

4

The movement screening

We watch you move. It shows us where there is pain or an old injury to work around, and which exercises actually suit you. This is an assessment, not a diagnosis.

5

Your recommendation

Built from the scan, the screening and everything you told us at the start. A plan written to reach your goal in the shortest amount of time possible.

6

Options, then your first session

We go through the ways to get there and what each one costs. If it is a fit, we book your first session and there is a short bit of paperwork. After that you are training.

Most people can tell inside five minutes whether they are talking to a professional. The terminology, the questions, the way the room is run. By halfway through, you will know whether this is someone with the experience to get you to your goal.

Questions

Straight answers to what people ask before booking.

Do I need to be in shape before I start?

You can start right where you are. We choose exercises and weights that make sense for your current strength and experience, and the movement assessment at the consultation is there to find the right starting point rather than to test you.

Will I feel comfortable walking in?

It is a private 1,500 square foot studio, not a big-box gym floor. Most of the time it is you and your coach. Nobody is queuing for the rack you are using and nobody is watching you learn a new movement.

Can you help if I already work out?

Yes, and it is a common starting point. Bring in what you have been doing. We will look at where you are progressing, where you are stuck, and what to change first rather than wiping out everything that is already working.

Can I do this with my work schedule, family and dinners out?

The studio is open from 5:00 AM Monday through Saturday, so training fits before work, after work, or in whatever gap you actually have. Nutrition is built for a normal week, including what to order when you go out. If a week gets away from you, text your coach and we will decide what to do with the time left rather than writing the week off.

How will I know if it's working?

We look at your lifts and your body-composition trends alongside whatever matters to you. That might be how your clothes fit, how confidently you move, or something you can do in the gym today that you could not do a month ago.

How much does personal training cost in Placentia?

One-on-one coaching runs $60 to $90 a session, and that includes your coaching, programming, tracked lifts, body scans, nutrition and text access between sessions. Semi-private in a group of three or fewer starts at $180 a month. At the consultation we go over the total for the schedule you are actually considering. [CONFIRM: three-month commitment]

What does personal training cost in Yorba Linda, Brea or Fullerton?

Clients drive in from Yorba Linda, Brea, Fullerton and Anaheim, and the pricing is the same wherever you come from: $60 to $90 a session one-on-one, semi-private from $180 a month. If you would rather train at home, in-home sessions are $150 with mileage included.
