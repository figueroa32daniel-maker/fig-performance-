How it works

Three steps to get started

Fig Performance is a private strength gym in Placentia for adults over 40, with a coach on the floor whenever we’re open. Here’s how you start.

  1. 01

    Book a free consultation

    About an hour with Dan. We talk about your goals, do an InBody scan and a workout assessment, and you leave with a game plan. You don’t have to sign up for anything.

  2. 02

    Pick how you want to train

  3. 03

    Show up and get stronger

    Coached sessions take 50 minutes: warm-up, workout and cool-down, then you’re out the door. About Fig Performance

What is Fig Performance?

Fig Performance is a private, coach-led strength gym for adults over 40 in Placentia, California. The gym is about 1,500 sq ft, a coach is on the floor whenever it's open, and it never gets crowded. There are three ways to train: one-on-one personal training with Coach Dan ($90 a session), semi-private small-group training ($199 to $345 a month) or a membership with open gym ($79.99 a month). The legal business name is Train With Dan Inc.

How does Fig Performance work?

It starts with a free consultation: about an hour with Dan for a talk about your goals, an InBody scan, a workout assessment and a game plan. Then you pick how you want to train: one-on-one personal training, semi-private small-group training or an open-gym membership. One-on-one and semi-private sessions are 50 minutes (warm-up, workout, cool-down) and come with a nutrition program, a training program in our app and a weekly InBody scan. They start with a 3-month commitment, because steady work is what gets results, and you can pause with notice. Membership is month to month.

Do you offer private personal training?

Yes, we offer private personal training, one-on-one with Coach Dan, either at our Placentia studio or at your home gym through in-home personal training. You get his full attention for the whole session. Rates are posted on our pricing page, and the first consult is free.

Who is the coach?

Coach Dan is the owner and head coach of Fig Performance. He's an ACSM certified personal trainer with a Precision Nutrition Level 1 certification, and he also holds CPR, AED, and first aid certifications. He's a former lifeguard, too. He has been training since age 15½ and has competed in four bodybuilding shows (three men's physique, one classic bodybuilding), earning first and second place finishes. Every personal training client works with him directly.

Where is Fig Performance located?

Fig Performance is at 1284 E. Yorba Linda Blvd, Placentia, CA 92870. It is a convenient option for people in Placentia and nearby Yorba Linda, Fullerton and Brea who want a coach-led gym instead of a big-box chain. Call or text (714) 396-7178 for directions or to book a visit.

What are Fig Performance's hours?

Fig Performance is open Monday through Friday from 5 AM to 8 PM and Saturday from 5 AM to 12 PM, and is closed on Sunday. The gym is staffed during all open hours, so early-morning, midday and evening training are all available. For holiday hours, check the Google Business Profile or call or text (714) 396-7178 before you visit.

Pricing & membership

How much does personal training cost at Fig Performance?

Personal training at Fig Performance is $90 per session, one-on-one with Coach Dan at the Placentia gym. Weekly and monthly rates for each number of sessions per week are on our pricing page.

Does membership include personal training?

No. Membership ($79.99 a month plus a $50 yearly maintenance fee, month to month, cancel anytime) covers open gym during staffed hours (Monday to Friday, 5 AM to 8 PM, and Saturday, 5 AM to 12 PM) and a free consultation with Dan: an InBody scan, a workout assessment and a recommended program for your goals. Members can try one semi-private session to see if it's for them. Coached sessions aren't part of it. Add semi-private small-group training or one-on-one personal training with Dan anytime.

Does Fig Performance have red light therapy in Placentia?

Yes. Red light therapy and vibration therapy both come with Fig Performance's semi-private training in Placentia, from $199 a month.

What’s included with each option?

Personal training ($90 a session, billed weekly or monthly with a 3-month minimum; $72 a session when you pay for 3 months upfront): 50-minute one-on-one sessions with Dan, a nutrition program, a training program in our app and a weekly InBody scan.

Semi-private ($199 a month for 1 coached group session a week, $225 for 2, $279 for 3, $345 for 4): 50-minute small-group sessions, a nutrition program, a training program in our app, a weekly InBody scan, open gym, and red light and vibration therapy. Membership ($79.99 a month plus a $50 yearly maintenance fee, month to month, cancel anytime): open gym during staffed hours and a free consultation with Dan. Personal training and semi-private have a 3-month minimum and you can pause with notice. No initiation fee on any plan.

Do you offer concierge personal training?

Yes. We call it in-home personal training: Dan comes to your home gym for private one-on-one sessions and brings gym accessories. It covers North Orange County and surrounding areas, including the 13 cities listed in the Visit section above. It starts with a free phone call, then an assessment at your home. Details are on our in-home personal training page.

Is it worth the money?

Personal training is $90 a session, billed weekly or monthly with a 3-month minimum, or save 20% when you pay in full for 3 months of coaching.

What you get is a coach focused only on you. The consult is free, so you can see the space, meet Dan and decide for yourself.

Does Fig Performance take insurance or HSA/FSA cards?

Fig Performance doesn't bill health insurance. If your own doctor writes a letter of medical necessity, you may be able to pay with HSA or FSA funds, and we're happy to provide a receipt for your plan, with no promise of reimbursement. Some employers also offer wellness or fitness stipends. Check with your plan administrator before paying.

Getting started

What happens at the free consultation?

Every new member, and anyone thinking about joining, gets a free consultation. You share the goals that matter most to you, complete an InBody scan and work through a few movements chosen around those goals. Dan then lays out a plan: how often to train and what the first weeks look like.

How does the free consult work?

Answer a few quick questions below and leave your number. Dan will reach out by text or call to set it up. The consult takes about an hour.

Personal training leads get a consult this week. Membership leads get a tour during pre-opening week.

What happens in my first personal training session?

Your first workout at Fig Performance is a strength assessment. We take you through a warm-up, the strength assessment, and a cool-down. Then we review how it all works together: how the training works, how nutrition works, the accountability, the InBody scans, red light therapy, and vibration therapy. Every client is re-tested with the strength assessment once a month, so we can make sure you're on the right path.

I don’t know if this will work for me. How do I find out?

That's what the free consult is for. You'll see what training here would look like before you commit to anything.

Progress gets measured: you start with an InBody body-composition scan and re-scan to see what's changing.

I don’t have time. How does this fit a busy schedule?

The facility is staffed Monday to Friday, 5 AM to 8 PM, and Saturday, 5 AM to 12 PM, so you can train before work, at lunch, or after work. Personal training is booked by appointment around your calendar.

With no crowds and no lines for equipment, the time you do have goes into training.

I have back, knee, hip or shoulder pain. Can I still train?

Tell Dan about it in the quiz or at your consult so it's part of your plan from the first session. If you're under a doctor's care for an injury, check with your doctor before starting a new program.
