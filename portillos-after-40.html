Stop letting them dress the salad at Portillo's. It is 340 calories and two grams of protein.

A Classic Caesar Salad with grilled chicken at Portillo's is 328 calories with 37 grams of protein. The same salad with the dressing on it is 668 calories with 39 grams. The dressing costs you 340 calories and buys you two grams of protein. That is true of every salad on their menu, and the number barely moves.

The dressing is 340 calories, every time

From Portillo's own nutrition document, dressed against undressed:

  • Caesar with grilled chicken: 328 cal / 37 g without, 668 cal / 39 g with
  • Chopped Salad: 506 cal / 42 g without, 846 cal / 42 g with
  • Classic Caesar: 200 cal / 12 g without, 540 cal / 14 g with
  • Spicy Chicken Chopped: 739 cal / 45 g without, 1,079 cal / 45 g with

Three hundred and forty calories, four times, almost exactly. Ask for it on the side and use a third of it and you have saved yourself more than two hundred calories without noticing.

The Caesar with grilled chicken is the order

Thirty-seven grams of protein for 328 calories and 826 mg of sodium is a genuinely excellent number, and it is sitting on the menu of a place famous for Italian beef and chili dogs.

The Greek Salad with chicken is the next best at 325 calories with 27 grams. The Chopped Salad carries the most protein at 42 grams, but it costs 506 calories undressed because of what else is in it.

The hot dogs are smaller than they feel

  • Hot Dog with Everything, regular: 352 calories, 13 g protein, 1,572 mg sodium
  • Hot Dog with Everything, jumbo: 462 calories, 19 g protein, 2,062 mg sodium
  • Chili Cheese Dog, regular: 511 calories, 22 g protein, 1,649 mg sodium

Three hundred and fifty calories for a hot dog is not the disaster people assume. The sodium is. A regular dog with everything carries over 1,500 mg, and the jumbo clears 2,000, which is most of a day in one item that takes four minutes to eat.

Italian beef, and the broiled chicken nobody orders

  • Italian Beef, regular: 693 calories, 33 g protein, 1,237 mg sodium
  • Italian Beef, Big Beef: 1,040 calories, 50 g protein, 1,856 mg sodium
  • Broiled Chicken Sandwich: 406 calories, 769 mg sodium
  • Breaded Chicken Sandwich: 601 calories, 2,058 mg sodium

Look at those last two together. Broiled against breaded is 195 calories and, more importantly, around 1,290 mg of sodium. The broiled chicken sandwich is the lowest sodium main on the entire menu and almost nobody orders it, because you do not go to Portillo's for broiled chicken.

Where it tops out

A Triple Cheeseburger is 1,419 calories with about 3,545 mg of sodium, and a double Rodeo Burger is 1,164 calories with 3,000 mg. Those are day-and-a-half sodium figures in a single sandwich.

What I would order

  1. Classic Caesar with grilled chicken, dressing on the side. 328 calories and 37 g of protein before you add any of it back.
  2. Broiled chicken sandwich if you want a sandwich. 406 calories and the lowest sodium main here.
  3. A regular dog with everything over a jumbo or a chili cheese. 352 calories, and the sodium is the reason not the fat.
  4. Dressing on the side, always. It is 340 calories at this restaurant no matter which salad you picked.

The 15-second version

If you remember one thing

  • Dressing adds 340 calories to every salad on the menu
  • Caesar with grilled chicken, dry: 328 cal and 37 g protein
  • Broiled chicken sandwich is the lowest sodium main

Why this one is on the list

Because the Buena Park location is fifteen minutes from the studio and people treat the whole place as a write-off day. It does not have to be. There is a 328 calorie, 37 gram salad on that menu, and the single decision that matters most is one you make while they are still building it.

A note on these numbers

Every figure above comes from Portillo's own Nutrition and Allergen document, dated September 2026. Three things were deliberately left out. The protein and carbohydrate columns for burgers and chicken sandwiches were not legible in the source, so only calories, fat and sodium are quoted for those. The sodium column for fries was likewise unreadable. And the document lists a Jumbo fries figure of 2,415 calories, which does not reconcile with the regular at 340 and the large at 483 and looks like an error on the page, so it is not quoted here.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What is the healthiest thing to order at Portillo's?

The Classic Caesar Salad with grilled chicken, undressed, at 328 calories with 37 grams of protein and 826 mg of sodium. Ask for the dressing on the side, because it adds 340 calories.

How many calories is Portillo's salad dressing?

About 340 calories, and it is remarkably consistent across the menu. It adds 340 to the Caesar with chicken, 340 to the Chopped Salad, 340 to the plain Caesar and 340 to the Spicy Chicken Chopped. It also adds almost no protein.

How many calories is a Portillo's hot dog?

A regular Hot Dog with Everything is 352 calories with 13 grams of protein. The jumbo is 462 with 19 grams. The number to watch is sodium, not calories: the regular carries about 1,572 mg and the jumbo about 2,062 mg.

How many calories is an Italian beef at Portillo's?

A regular Italian Beef sandwich is 693 calories with 33 grams of protein and about 1,237 mg of sodium. The Big Beef is 1,040 calories with 50 grams of protein.

What is the lowest sodium item at Portillo's?

Among the mains, the Broiled Chicken Sandwich at about 769 mg, which is roughly 1,290 mg less than the breaded version of the same sandwich. The undressed Classic Caesar is lower still at 270 mg, but without chicken it is not a meal.
