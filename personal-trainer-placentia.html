Fig Performance is a private, one-on-one strength studio at 1284 E. Yorba Linda Blvd in Placentia. Not a gym floor you walk onto alone. A room where one coach trains one person at a time, built for adults over 40 who are already doing the work and want it to start showing.

Who trains here

Placentia is a town of people who have been busy for thirty years: business owners, teachers, nurses, people in the trades, parents whose kids have left and who suddenly have an hour back. The ones who come to us usually arrive with some version of the same sentence. I used to be in shape. I still move. Nothing is changing.

  • You want to know what is actually happening. An InBody scan separates fat from muscle, so the last six months stop being a guess.
  • You want to be strong, not just lighter. Teresa started here at 60 and did her first pull-up about three months later. Sam is 70 and deadlifts 135 pounds for reps.
  • You want to keep what you like doing. Golf, hiking, grandkids, carrying your own bags. Jonathan had quit golf with a back that hurt to bend. He plays again.

A Placentia gym, not a chain

We are on E. Yorba Linda Blvd, a few minutes from anywhere in town: Tri-City Park, the Alta Vista and Kraemer neighbourhoods, Valencia and El Dorado. There is parking at the door. Most clients are here and gone inside an hour.

What that means in practice is that there is no waiting for a rack, nobody watching you learn something new, and no hand-off to a junior trainer. Every session is with Coach Dan.

What training looks like

  1. Free consult and InBody scan. About an hour: body fat, muscle and visceral fat, a movement and strength assessment, and your goals.
  2. Your plan. Two or more 50-minute sessions a week, one-on-one with Coach Dan, plus a simple nutrition plan built from foods you already eat.
  3. Recover. Clients use red light therapy and vibration therapy as part of the routine.
  4. Re-scan. The InBody shows what changed. Then we adjust. Scan. Plan. Train. Recover. Re-scan.

Coach Dan Figueroa is an ACSM certified personal trainer and a Precision Nutrition Level 1 certified nutrition coach. Every session is with him. No hand-offs to a junior trainer.
