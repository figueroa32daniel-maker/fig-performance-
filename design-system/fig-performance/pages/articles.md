# Page override: articles and guides (Editorial / Magazine)

Applies to every guide built with `page(..., kind="article")`, like the fitness-after-40 guides and workout plans. These rules override `MASTER.md` where they differ. Everything else in MASTER.md still applies.

**Goal:** articles should read like a premium health magazine written by a real coach, not a wall of text.

## Layout

- Article header: kicker, then the H1 in **title case or sentence case** (never ALL CAPS), a one-line dek, then the byline ("By Coach Dan Figueroa, ACSM CPT · Precision Nutrition Level 1") and a human date ("Updated Nov 3, 2026").
- Featured image right under the header (Dan coaching, the gym, or the exercise in the article). Full width of the text column, 3:2 or 16:9.
- Reading column about 680–720px wide, body text 17–18px with line-height 1.65.
- Keep the "On this page" contents box, but make it lighter: no heavy card, just a hairline and the gold rule.

## Editorial elements (use 2–4 per article)

- **Client callout:** a highlighted box for a real client result, with name, age and 2–3 numbers. Example: "Teresa, 60: first pull-up in about 3 months. Now benches about 85 lb for reps." Cream background, gold left border.
- **Pull quote:** one line of Dan's own words, large Montserrat, gold rule above. Example: "Chase progress, not perfection."
- **Key takeaway box** near the top: 3 bullets of the answer.
- **In-body images:** an exercise photo or demo every 3–4 sections when available.
- **Stat highlights:** a big number with a short label (e.g. "5,400+ steps a day").

## Avoid

- More than one callout or quote in a row
- Stock images
- Long ALL CAPS headings
- Repeating the same CTA box more than twice in one article
