Looking for a personal trainer in Brea? Fig Performance is a private, one-on-one strength gym at 1284 E. Yorba Linda Blvd in Placentia, a quick drive south from Brea down Kraemer Blvd or the 57. It's built for adults over 40 with demanding jobs who want to lose fat, build strength and stop wondering whether their workouts are working.

Who we help in Brea

Brea is where a lot of North Orange County goes to work. If you spend your days in an office or a business park, in meetings or on the 57, your energy is usually spent by the time the gym would normally happen. We help people in that spot:

  • Train before work, not after. Doors open at 5 AM, Monday through Saturday. A 50-minute session fits before the commute.
  • Undo a desk. Tight hips, a stiff upper back and a cranky lower back are the usual starting point. The plan starts there.
  • See a number move. Many clients haven't lost weight in years despite "eating pretty healthy." The InBody scan shows whether it's fat, muscle or water, and this guide explains the most common reasons the scale is stuck.

Why Brea clients drive to Placentia

Brea has big gyms. What it doesn't have much of is a private floor where the only two people in the room are you and your coach. At Fig Performance there's no waiting for a squat rack at 5:30 PM, no being watched, and no coach splitting attention between four clients. Progress is measured on an InBody scan, so you know in 90 days whether the plan is working.

What training looks like

  1. Free consult and InBody scan. About an hour: body fat, muscle and visceral fat, a movement and strength assessment, and your goals.
  2. Your plan. Two or more 50-minute sessions a week, one-on-one with Coach Dan, plus a simple nutrition plan built from foods you already eat.
  3. Recover. Members use red light therapy and vibration therapy as part of the routine.
  4. Re-scan. The InBody shows what changed. Then we adjust. Scan. Plan. Train. Recover. Re-scan.

Coach Dan Figueroa is an ACSM certified personal trainer and a Precision Nutrition Level 1 certified nutrition coach. Every session is with him. No hand-offs to a junior trainer.
