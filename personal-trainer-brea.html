Ready to start? Book a free consult

Looking for a personal trainer in Brea? Fig Performance is a private, one-on-one strength gym on E. Yorba Linda Blvd in Placentia, a short drive from Brea. It's built for adults over 40 who already exercise and eat reasonably well, and want the effort to finally show.

Who we help

Many of our clients live or work around Brea and spend long days at a desk or on the road. They want to feel strong and look the part again, without spending hours in a crowded gym. We help them:

  • Lose fat and build muscle at the same time, measured with an InBody body-composition scan.
  • Get stronger without getting hurt, with a plan built around your lower back, knees, hips or shoulders.
  • Fit training around a demanding schedule: we're staffed Monday to Friday, 5 AM to 8 PM, and Saturday, 5 AM to 12 PM.

What makes Fig Performance different

  • One-on-one with Coach Dan. Every personal training session is just you and an ACSM certified trainer. No hand-offs.
  • No crowds, no waiting. A private 1,500 sq ft facility instead of a packed big-box floor.
  • Measured, not guessed. Scan. Plan. Train. Recover. Re-scan.
  • Recovery built in. Members get red light therapy and vibration therapy.
  • Concierge option. Prefer to train at your Brea home or office? Coach Dan can come to you. Ask for a quote.

Pricing

Personal training is $90 a session, billed weekly or monthly, with a 3-month minimum. Save 20% when you pay in full for 3 months. Membership with classes and recovery tools is also available. See plans and pricing.

How to start

Book a free consult. It takes about an hour: an InBody scan, a movement assessment, a conversation about your goals, and a game plan. You'll know exactly what training would look like before you commit to anything.
