Biography

Daniel Figueroa is a personal trainer and the founder and head coach of FIG PERFORMANCE, a private strength-training studio in Placentia, California. He works with adults over 40 on strength, body composition and long-term training habits.

Daniel began lifting as a teenager and has participated in four bodybuilding competitions, including men's physique and classic bodybuilding. His competition history includes first- and second-place finishes. He started coaching clients through Train With Dan before opening FIG PERFORMANCE's Placentia studio in November 2026.

His professional background also includes CPR, AED and first aid training, and work as a lifeguard. He writes practical fitness and nutrition guides for people managing training alongside work, travel and family schedules.

His coaching approach

At FIG PERFORMANCE, Daniel combines private one-on-one strength training with an initial assessment and ongoing body-composition tracking using InBody scans. He also helps clients make nutrition changes that fit their existing routines. The Placentia studio serves people willing to travel from nearby communities including Yorba Linda, Brea and Fullerton.

For Daniel, the focus is a plan someone can continue using, rather than a short-term challenge. Read about the personal training approach, nutrition coaching and InBody progress tracking.
