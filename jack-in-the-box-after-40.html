Unpopular opinion: Jack in the Box has two menus, and one of them is fine.

Grilled chicken strips: about 200 calories and roughly 30 grams of protein.

Jack in the Box is running two different restaurants out of the same window. One of them is fried, enormous and open at two in the morning on purpose. The other is grilled chicken, and it is genuinely fine. The gap between those two menus is the widest of any chain in these guides, and which one you are eating from comes down to about four words at the speaker.

The two menus

On the grilled side, the numbers land roughly here:

  • Grilled Chicken Sandwich: around 500 calories, roughly 33 g protein
  • Grilled chicken strips, 4 piece: about 200 calories, roughly 30 g protein
  • Grilled Chicken Salad: about 320 calories, roughly 32 g protein

Thirty grams of protein for 200 calories in the strips is a serious number. It is in the same bracket as the Chick-fil-A grilled nuggets, at a restaurant nobody thinks of this way.

Grilled over crispy is worth about 200 calories

Across the menu, choosing the grilled version of a chicken item saves somewhere in the region of 150 to 200 calories against the crispy version of the same thing. The Homestyle Ranch Chicken Club sits near 700 calories; the Grilled Chicken Sandwich is near 500.

That is one word at the order screen for the same amount of chicken. It is the Chick-fil-A and KFC lesson again, and it keeps being true because breading is just flour and oil wrapped around the thing you actually wanted.

Two menus, one window. The grilled one is fine. Nobody told you it was there.

The number I am not going to give you

Sodium. Published figures for the same Grilled Chicken Sandwich range from about 900 mg to roughly 1,440 mg depending on which source you read, and I am not going to pick one and present it as fact.

What is safe to say: this is a fast food restaurant, the sauces and the bread carry salt, and the grilled items sit meaningfully below the fried burgers and combos. If you are managing blood pressure, the strips and the salad are the safer end of the menu, and asking for sauce on the side is free.

The two in the morning problem

This restaurant is open when nothing else is, and that is when the worst decisions get made. If you are standing there late, the useful thing to know is that four grilled chicken strips exist, they are around 200 calories, and they will do the job of stopping you being hungry without costing you the day.

That is not a lecture about not going. It is the single order worth memorising for the night you end up there anyway.

What I would order

  1. Grilled chicken strips. About 200 calories, roughly 30 g protein. Best item on the board.
  2. Grilled Chicken Salad if you want a full meal. Around 320 calories with about 32 g protein.
  3. Say grilled, not crispy. Worth roughly 150 to 200 calories for the same chicken.
  4. Sauce on the side, since the sodium figures here are all over the place and that is the lever you control.

The 15-second version

If you remember one thing

  • Grilled strips: about 200 cal and 30 g protein
  • Grilled over crispy saves roughly 200 calories
  • Sodium figures here disagree, keep sauce on the side

Why this one is on the list

Because this is the restaurant people end up at rather than choose, usually late, usually hungry, usually without a plan. Every other guide on this site is about ordering well somewhere you decided to go. This one is about having a default for the place you did not decide to go.

A note on these numbers

This page is the least precisely sourced in the series and it says so rather than hiding it. Published figures for the Jack in the Box Grilled Chicken Sandwich range from 430 to 600 calories and from 20 to 33 grams of protein across different databases, and sodium for that one item appears at 900, 1,000, 1,090 and 1,440 mg. Where sources cluster, around 500 calories and roughly 33 grams of protein, this page says approximately. Where they do not cluster, as with sodium, this page gives no figure at all. Treat everything here as a guide to which tier of the menu to order from rather than as exact macros, and check the chain's own current nutrition information if you are tracking tightly.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What is the healthiest thing at Jack in the Box?

Grilled chicken strips, at about 200 calories for roughly 30 grams of protein, are the best item on the menu. The Grilled Chicken Salad is the best full meal at around 320 calories with about 32 grams.

How much protein is in the Jack in the Box grilled chicken sandwich?

Sources cluster around 33 grams at roughly 500 calories, though published figures for this item vary more than most, from 430 to 600 calories and from 20 to 33 grams of protein. Treat it as approximate.

Is grilled better than crispy at Jack in the Box?

Yes, and by a meaningful margin. Choosing grilled over crispy saves somewhere around 150 to 200 calories for the same amount of chicken. The breading is flour and oil around the thing you actually wanted.

How much sodium is in Jack in the Box food?

Honestly, the published figures do not agree well enough to quote. The same grilled chicken sandwich appears anywhere from about 900 to roughly 1,440 mg depending on the source. What is safe to say is that the grilled items sit well below the fried burgers and combos, and asking for sauce on the side is free.

What should I order at Jack in the Box late at night?

Four grilled chicken strips, around 200 calories and roughly 30 grams of protein. It is the one order worth memorising for the nights you end up there without a plan.
