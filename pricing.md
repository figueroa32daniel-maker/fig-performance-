# Pricing — Fig Performance (Placentia, CA)

Last updated: November 24, 2026. Source of truth: https://www.figperformance.com/pricing

## Free consult with InBody scan
- Price: $0
- About an hour: InBody body-composition scan, movement and strength assessment, goals conversation, game plan
- No commitment. Book online: https://calendly.com/trainwithcoachdan/free-consult-inbody-scan

## Fig Strong 90: personal training (one-on-one with Coach Dan Figueroa, ACSM certified)
- A 90-day program to lose fat, get stronger and tone up
- Price: $60 to $90 per session, billed weekly or monthly
- Where you land in that range depends on how often you train: the more sessions a week, the lower the per-session rate
- Your exact rate is set at the free consultation
- 3-month minimum commitment on every plan; no drop-ins or one-off sessions
- Includes: 50-minute one-on-one sessions (warm-up, workout, cool-down), planned around your goals, schedule and fitness level; a nutrition program; a training program in the app; support from Dan Monday through Saturday; a weekly InBody scan
- Missed sessions are still billed; pause with notice for travel or a break

## Membership (open gym)
- Price: $79.99/month plus a $50 yearly maintenance fee, charged when you join
- Month to month, cancel anytime. No initiation fee.
- Includes: open gym during staffed hours (Mon–Fri 5 AM–8:30 PM, Sat 5 AM–12 PM) and a free consultation with Dan (InBody scan, workout assessment, recommended program for your goals)
- Members can try one semi-private session; add-ons (red light, vibration, weekly InBody scans) available on request
- Does not include coached sessions

## Semi-private (coached training in groups of 3 people max)
- Price: from $180/month, depending on how many group sessions a week
- Your exact rate is set at the free consultation
- No initiation fee. 3-month minimum, then month to month until you pause or cancel. Pause with notice (travel, a break). Otherwise missed sessions are still billed and don't carry over.
- Booked by appointment, sessions available all day, groups of 3 people max
- Includes: 50-minute coached small-group sessions (warm-up, workout, cool-down), a nutrition program, a training program in the Fig app, a weekly InBody scan, open gym during staffed hours, red light therapy, vibration therapy
- Does not include weekly one-on-one personal training

## In-home personal training
- Coach Dan trains you one-on-one in your own home gym across North Orange County and nearby cities
- Starts with a free phone call, then an assessment at your home
- Price: quoted by location
- Details: https://www.figperformance.com/in-home-personal-training
