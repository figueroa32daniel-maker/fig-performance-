Looking for a personal trainer in Villa Park? Fig Performance is a private, one-on-one strength gym in Placentia, a short drive north of the Hidden Jewel. Villa Park is almost entirely single-family homes, so there is no real gym inside the 2.1 square miles of the city itself, which is why most residents already drive out to train. We are built for the adults over 40 who do: people who exercise, eat reasonably well, and still can't get the effort to show on the scan.

Who we help in Villa Park

Villa Park is the smallest city in Orange County and one of its most affluent, a quiet enclave of large lots, horse properties and executives who have done well. The people Fig Performance is built for live exactly this way: business owners and professionals in their 40s, 50s and 60s, with the time and the means to train properly, who are tired of guessing. The pattern we hear most is "I'm doing everything right and nothing is changing." Usually something is changing, just not the number on the scale. We help them:

  • See what's actually happening. An InBody scan separates fat from muscle, so you stop wondering whether the last six months of effort did anything.
  • Train for the life Villa Park is built around. Keeping up with the property, riding, golf, hiking the nearby Santiago Oaks and Irvine Regional Park trails. Strength in the hips, back and shoulders is what protects all of it.
  • Lift with a plan, not a guess. Muscle after 40 responds to real progressive strength work. Here's how that looks after 50, and for women over 50.

Why Villa Park clients come to Placentia

Villa Park has no gym of its own, so the question was never whether to drive, only where to. People choose Fig Performance over the big clubs in Orange and Anaheim Hills for three reasons: every session is private and one-on-one, with no crowd and no waiting; progress is measured with an InBody scan instead of a mirror and a hope; and it fits a full schedule, with the doors open at 5 AM on weekdays and on Saturday mornings.

What training looks like

  1. Free consult and InBody scan. About an hour: body fat, muscle and visceral fat, a movement and strength assessment, and your goals.
  2. Your plan. Two or more 50-minute sessions a week, one-on-one with Coach Dan, plus a simple nutrition plan built from foods you already eat.
  3. Recover. Clients use red light therapy and vibration therapy as part of the routine.
  4. Re-scan. The InBody shows what changed. Then we adjust. Scan. Plan. Train. Recover. Re-scan.

Coach Dan Figueroa is an ACSM certified personal trainer and a Precision Nutrition Level 1 certified nutrition coach. Every session is with him. No hand-offs to a junior trainer.
