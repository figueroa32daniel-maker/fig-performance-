          Your week

          Client sessions and consults share this schedule. Any hour with a session on it is hidden from the website, so nobody can book a consult over a client.

          Clients

          Your training clients. Add them once, then put them on the schedule or text them from here. Give a client a fixed weekly time and it goes on your calendar by itself, three weeks ahead. Sessions are one hour.

          Consults

          Settings

          Consult hours

          The window for consults each day. A consult blocks 90 minutes, start times are offered every 30 minutes, and each one has to finish by the end time. Hours with a client session on them are skipped automatically. Leave a day on Closed to take no bookings.

          Days off

          No bookings on these dates.

            Follow-up texts

            Sent after a consult is marked “Showed” or “No-show”. If you don’t mark it, it counts as “Showed” the next morning (tap No-show under Consults to correct it). Someone already on your Clients list gets none. Only sent to people who ticked the optional texts box, never to anyone who replied STOP, and only between 9 AM and 8 PM. They stop as soon as the person replies or you mark them Signed up or Not interested. Use {first} for their first name. “Reply STOP to opt out” is added if you leave it off.

            Client session reminders

            Missed calls

            When someone calls the gym’s texting number and you can’t pick up, they get this text once, and you get a text saying who called. Nobody who replied STOP is texted.

            Google reviews

            Paste your Google review link here and an “Ask for a Google review” button appears next to each client. It sends one text, only when you press it, and never twice to the same person within 6 months.

            Texting assistant

            When this is on, the assistant answers every text for you. Clients on your Clients list can book, move and cancel their own sessions (never two people in the same hour). A cancel inside 24 hours needs their yes and counts as a used session. New people get answers from the facts below and a link to book a free consult. Anything it can’t answer waits for you on the Today page.

            Text my phone

            The assistant remembers when each client usually trains (from your last 8 weeks). Clients with a steady schedule are offered their usual times and just reply YES. Everyone else is asked what works. Clients with a fixed weekly time are never asked.
