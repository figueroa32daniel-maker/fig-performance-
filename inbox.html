    Your week

    Client sessions and consults share this schedule. Any hour with a session on it is hidden from the website, so nobody can book a consult over a client.

    Clients

    Your training clients. Add them once, then put them on the schedule or text them from here. Give a client a fixed weekly time and it goes on your calendar by itself, three weeks ahead. Sessions are one hour.

    Consults

    Settings

    Consult hours

    The window for consults each day. A consult blocks 90 minutes, start times are offered every 30 minutes, and each one has to finish by the end time. Hours with a client session on them are skipped automatically. Leave a day on Closed to take no bookings.

    Days off

    No bookings on these dates.

      Follow-up texts

      Sent after a consult you mark “Showed” or “No-show”, only to people who ticked the optional texts box, never to anyone who replied STOP, and only between 9 AM and 8 PM. They stop as soon as the person replies or you mark them Signed up or Not interested. Use {first} for their first name. “Reply STOP to opt out” is added if you leave it off.

      Texting assistant

      When this is on, clients on your Clients list can text the days and times they want and the assistant puts them on your Week if the hour is free. It offers other times when it is taken, sends anything else to you, and texts you everything it does. It never changes a session inside 24 hours.

      The assistant remembers when each client usually trains (from your last 8 weeks). Clients with a steady schedule are offered their usual times and just reply YES. Everyone else is asked what works. Clients with a fixed weekly time are never asked.

      Not connected yet: the assistant needs its AI key added in Vercel before it can answer.

      Your calendar

      Subscribe to this private link in Apple Calendar (File → New Calendar Subscription) or Google Calendar (Other calendars → From URL) and every consult shows up on your own calendar. Don’t share the link.
