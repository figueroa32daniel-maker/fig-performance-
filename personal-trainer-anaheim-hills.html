Who we help in Anaheim Hills

If you live in Anaheim Hills, odds are you have two things going on: long days, often with a commute on the 91, and an active life you want to protect. Hiking the canyon trails, golf, cycling, chasing kids or grandkids. The goal is rarely just to look better. It's to keep doing those things at 55, 65 and 75. We help them:

  • Build strength that carries over. Squats, hinges, presses, carries and pull-ups, loaded progressively. Here's what that looks like for clients in their 70s.
  • Protect knees, hips and backs. Hills are hard on joints. Training is built around the ones that complain.
  • Make a limited schedule count. Two 50-minute one-on-one sessions a week, planned and tracked, do more than five unplanned hours at a crowded gym.

Why Anaheim Hills clients drive to Placentia

It's a private gym, which is rare on this side of the county. The only people on the floor are you and Coach Dan. No waiting, no crowd, no coach splitting attention. Progress is measured with an InBody scan, so fat and muscle are tracked separately and you know whether the plan is working before you've spent a year on it.

What training looks like

  1. Free consult and InBody scan. About an hour: body fat, muscle and visceral fat, a movement and strength assessment, and your goals.
  2. Your plan. Two or more 50-minute sessions a week, one-on-one with Coach Dan, plus a simple nutrition plan built from foods you already eat.
  3. Recover. Members use red light therapy and vibration therapy as part of the routine.
  4. Re-scan. The InBody shows what changed. Then we adjust. Scan. Plan. Train. Recover. Re-scan.

Coach Dan Figueroa is an ACSM certified personal trainer and a Precision Nutrition Level 1 certified nutrition coach. Every session is with him. No hand-offs to a junior trainer.
