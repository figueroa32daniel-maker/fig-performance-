How to get 32 grams of protein at Five Guys for 440 calories and almost no salt.

Take the bun off. They do it well, and the bun is 240 calories.

A bunless hamburger at Five Guys is about 440 calories with roughly 32 grams of protein and around 100 mg of sodium. That last number is lower than almost anything else in these guides, and most people have no idea, because they have never thought of Five Guys as anything other than the place with the enormous fries.

The bun is 240 calories and they will leave it off

Five Guys will build any sandwich as a bowl or wrapped in lettuce, and they are unusually good at it. The bun is worth about 240 calories and somewhere around 35 to 39 grams of carbohydrate.

  • Bunless Hamburger: about 440 calories, roughly 32 g protein, around 100 mg sodium
  • Bunless Little Hamburger: about 220 calories, roughly 16 g protein, around 50 mg sodium

Sixteen grams of protein for 220 calories and 50 mg of salt is a better profile than most things people pack in a lunch box, and it comes off a flat-top in a burger restaurant.

Little means one patty, not a small burger

This is the thing people get wrong at the counter. A "Little" Hamburger is not a smaller burger, it is a single patty burger. The regular Hamburger comes with two.

  • Little Hamburger: roughly 480 to 540 calories, about 23 g protein, around 380 mg sodium
  • Hamburger (two patties): roughly 700 to 840 calories, about 39 g protein, around 430 mg sodium

One patty is about 300 calories and 16 grams of protein. If you are ordering out of habit rather than hunger, Little is almost always the right call.

The surprise: this is a low sodium restaurant

Five Guys burgers run around 380 to 430 mg of sodium. Bunless they drop to 50 to 100. Compare that with a Jersey Mike's regular sub reaching toward 3,530 mg, a Del Taco Epic burrito at 3,130, or a Panera bread bowl near 2,170.

If a doctor has talked to you about blood pressure, Five Guys is a far better place to end up than its reputation suggests. The fat and the calories are real. The salt is not the issue here.

The fries are the entire problem

I am not going to put a number on them, because the published figures vary and a Five Guys fry order is famously not a standard portion. What I will tell you is that a regular order is one of the largest single servings of fries in American fast food, frequently reported at somewhere near a thousand calories, and that it is routinely more than the burger it came with.

Order the smallest size they have, or skip them, or split one. The burger is not what makes this restaurant expensive in calories.

What I would order

  1. Bunless hamburger or a burger bowl. About 440 calories, roughly 32 g protein, very low sodium.
  2. Pile on the free vegetables. Lettuce, tomato, grilled onions, grilled mushrooms, peppers. All free, all basically no calories.
  3. Little, not regular, if you want the bun. One patty instead of two.
  4. Sort out the fries before you order, not after they arrive. That decision is the meal.

The 15-second version

If you remember one thing

  • Bunless or bowl: the bun is about 240 calories
  • Little means one patty, not a small burger
  • The fries are bigger than the burger

Why this one is on the list

Because Five Guys has a reputation as the indulgent choice, and half of that reputation is earned by the fries alone. The burger underneath is grilled beef with free vegetables and almost no salt. Take the bun off and it is one of the cleaner things you can order in a strip mall.

The clients who struggle are not the ones who go to Five Guys. They are the ones who go and order on autopilot, which here means two patties, a bun and a bag of fries bigger than the sandwich.

A note on these numbers

Five Guys toppings are free and unlimited, which is exactly why published calorie figures for the same burger disagree. The Little Hamburger appears at 480 calories at some sources and 540 at others, and one states plainly that the lower figure is without heavy toppings. Protein at about 23 grams and sodium at about 380 mg agree across four independent sources, so those are given firmly and the calories are given as a range. The bunless figures above each come from two sources that agree. The fries are deliberately not quoted, because the published numbers vary and the portion genuinely is not standard.

Same idea every time: find the protein, spot the one item doing the damage, and order so you are not eating differently from everyone else at the table.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Can you order a burger without the bun at Five Guys?

Yes. They will serve any sandwich as a bowl or wrapped in lettuce, and they do it well. The bun is worth about 240 calories and roughly 35 to 39 grams of carbohydrate, so it is a meaningful saving for one sentence at the counter.

How much protein is in a Five Guys burger?

A Little Hamburger, which is one patty, has about 23 grams. The regular Hamburger has two patties and about 39 grams. A bunless hamburger is roughly 440 calories with about 32 grams of protein.

What is the difference between a Little Hamburger and a Hamburger at Five Guys?

The number of patties, not the size of the burger. Little means one patty, regular means two. That is roughly a 300 calorie difference for about 16 grams of protein.

Is Five Guys high in sodium?

No, and this surprises people. The burgers run around 380 to 430 mg, and bunless they drop to roughly 50 to 100 mg. That is far below a Jersey Mike's sub, a Del Taco Epic burrito or a Panera bread bowl. The calories are real at Five Guys, but the salt is not the problem.

Can I eat Five Guys on a diet?

Yes, if you handle the fries. A bunless burger with free vegetable toppings is around 440 calories and roughly 32 grams of protein. A regular order of fries is one of the largest in American fast food and is usually bigger than the burger, so decide about it before you order rather than after it arrives.
