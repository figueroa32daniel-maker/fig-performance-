3 Personal Training Client Checklist

FIG PERFORMANCE

Personal Training Client Checklist

Initial each item to show you have read and agree to it.

  1. 1. Commitment and Cancellation. Your personal training agreement includes a 3-month minimum commitment. If you choose to cancel after the commitment period, Fig Performance requires 30 days' written notice. If you cancel before the end of the commitment period, an early termination fee of 15% of the remaining balance applies. Any payments due within the 30-day notice period are due at the time of cancellation. Cancellations must be given in person to your trainer or by email to trainat@figperformance.com.

    Please initial item 1 (2 to 4 letters).

  2. 2. Billing. Your training is billed as outlined in your agreement (weekly or monthly) for as long as your program is active. Billing is not tied to how many sessions you attend. Sessions you purchase but do not use remain available for 6 months from the purchase date, as long as your agreement is active.

    Please initial item 2 (2 to 4 letters).

  3. 3. Agreement Conversion. At the end of your commitment period, your agreement automatically converts to week-to-week or month-to-month, matching your billing choice, until you cancel. You may cancel at any time after the commitment period by giving 30 days' notice before the next billing date. If you give less than 30 days' notice, the next scheduled payment is still due and the cancellation takes effect the following week or month.

    Please initial item 3 (2 to 4 letters).

  4. 4. 24-Hour Session Cancellation. Please cancel or reschedule a session at least 24 hours in advance by calling or texting (714) 396-7178. Sessions canceled with less than 24 hours' notice are forfeited.

    Please initial item 4 (2 to 4 letters).

  5. 5. Session Promise. Fig Performance is a private, one-on-one studio. If your trainer is unavailable for a session, we will offer a make-up session at a time that works for you, or a qualified substitute coach.

    Please initial item 5 (2 to 4 letters).

  6. 6. Health and Readiness. I will tell my trainer about any injury, medical condition, medication, or change in my health. I understand I should consult my physician before starting a training program, and I have signed the Fig Performance Liability Waiver.
