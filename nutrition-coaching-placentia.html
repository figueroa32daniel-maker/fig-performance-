How nutrition coaching works

  1. Nutrition questionnaire. Which proteins, carbs and fats you like, the foods you actually enjoy, and what a normal day of eating looks like for you.
  2. Structure what you already eat. Coach Dan raises your protein, sets your calories and builds a calorie deficit you can live with if fat loss is the goal.
  3. Your plan, with options. Breakfast A or B, lunch A or B, snack A or B, and so on. You don't need an app. If you like tracking, that's fine too.
  4. Measure with InBody. Body fat, muscle and visceral fat, not just your weight, so you know you're losing fat and keeping muscle.
  5. Adjust. Every week in your sessions, you talk through what's working and what isn't, and the plan changes with you.

Why nutrition changes after 40

After 40, you lose muscle more easily, and a desk job cuts how much you move. When you eat less to lose fat, your body can give up muscle along with it. Protein is what helps you keep it. That's why the plan starts with protein at every meal, pairs it with strength training, and adds daily walking (over 5,000 steps, ideally 6,000 or more). Nutrition coach or registered dietitian?

Coach Dan is a Precision Nutrition Level 1 certified nutrition coach and an ACSM certified personal trainer. He isn't a registered dietitian. Nutrition coaching at Fig Performance is for healthy adults who want to lose fat, build muscle and eat with more structure. It isn't medical nutrition therapy.

See a registered dietitian (RD) or your doctor if you have diabetes, kidney disease, a food allergy, a digestive condition, an eating disorder, or you're pregnant, or if your doctor has prescribed a special diet. Many clients work with both: their RD or doctor for the medical side, and Coach Dan for training, protein, habits and accountability.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Do you offer nutrition coaching in Placentia?

Yes. Fig Performance includes nutrition coaching with one-on-one personal training: a nutrition questionnaire, more protein, set calories, and a simple plan with rotating meal options, checked with InBody body-composition scans.

Do I have to track calories or macros?

No. Most clients follow a plan with rotating options, like breakfast A or B, built from foods they already like. If you enjoy tracking, you can.

Is Coach Dan a nutrition coach or a dietitian?

Coach Dan is a Precision Nutrition Level 1 certified nutrition coach and ACSM certified personal trainer, not a registered dietitian. For a medical condition, a food allergy, an eating disorder, pregnancy or a prescribed diet, see a registered dietitian or your doctor.

How much protein should I eat after 40?

Enough to keep the muscle you have while you lose fat. Start by putting a solid protein source in every meal. Your exact target depends on your body size, goals and health, which Coach Dan sets at your consult.
