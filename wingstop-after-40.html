How to get 60 grams of protein at Wingstop for 540 calories.

Six plain classic wings. The flavour you say next is the real decision.

Six plain classic wings at Wingstop are about 540 calories with roughly 60 grams of protein. That is a better protein number than almost anything in these guides. And the thing that decides whether that order is sensible or not is not how many wings you ordered. It is which flavour you said.

The flavour is the whole decision

Every classic bone-in wing carries about 10 grams of protein regardless of flavour. The calories move a little. The sodium moves enormously:

  • Plain: about 90 calories, 10 g protein, around 30 mg sodium
  • Garlic Parmesan: about 120 calories, 10 g protein, around 75 mg
  • Mango Habanero: about 100 calories, 10 g protein, around 80 mg
  • Hickory Smoked BBQ: about 100 calories, 10 g protein, around 150 mg
  • Original Hot: about 90 calories, 10 g protein, around 230 mg
  • Cajun: about 90 calories, 10 g protein, around 310 mg

Plain and Cajun are the same calories and the same protein. One carries ten times the sodium of the other. Across a ten piece order that is 300 mg against 3,100 mg, which is the difference between a sensible dinner and a full day of salt.

Classic beats boneless, and it is not close

Boneless wings are not wings. They are breaded chunks of breast meat, which changes everything:

  • A classic bone-in wing carries roughly twice the protein of a boneless wing
  • Boneless carry substantially more sodium per piece
  • Boneless bring carbohydrate with them from the breading, where bone-in wings have almost none

An eight piece boneless order in a sauced flavour can land near 590 calories with about 30 grams of protein and over 2,200 mg of sodium. Six plain classic wings give you double that protein for less salt than a slice of bread.

Plain and Cajun. Same calories, same protein, ten times the salt. Nobody reads the flavour as a nutrition decision.

The dips are a second meal

This is the Raising Cane's problem again. Ranch and blue cheese are not condiments at the volume they are served, and a ten wing order with ranch can push past 1,500 calories once the dip is counted.

Ask for it on the side, use half, or skip it. The wings themselves are among the cleanest protein in fast food. It is the cup next to them that moves the number.

What I would order

  1. Six to ten classic bone-in wings, plain or a dry rub. Six is about 540 calories and roughly 60 g of protein.
  2. Avoid Cajun and Original Hot if sodium matters. Same calories, several times the salt.
  3. Never boneless. Half the protein, more salt, and breading you did not want.
  4. Dip on the side. Ranch is the single biggest variable in the order after the flavour.

The 15-second version

If you remember one thing

  • Six plain classic wings: about 540 cal, 60 g protein
  • Plain is 30 mg sodium, Cajun is 310 mg, same wing
  • Boneless have half the protein of bone-in

Why this one is on the list

Because wings read as the indulgent order and they are quietly one of the best protein sources in fast food. Ten grams of protein for 90 calories is a ratio most things in a supermarket cannot match.

The whole restaurant hinges on two decisions nobody thinks of as decisions: which flavour, and whether you got a dip. Get those right and this is a genuinely good dinner for someone over 40 trying to hold muscle.

A note on these numbers

Per-wing figures here are well agreed. Four independent sources give a plain classic wing at 90 calories with 10 grams of protein and 30 mg of sodium, and agree flavour by flavour on the rest. One source reports a ten piece plain classic order at 700 calories and 52 grams of protein, which contradicts both its own per-wing figures and the other four sources, so it was discarded rather than averaged in. Wing sizes vary in a way that pizza slices do, so treat totals as close rather than exact.

FAQ

Straight answers.

How much protein is in a Wingstop wing?

About 10 grams in a classic bone-in wing, regardless of flavour, at roughly 90 to 120 calories. Six plain classic wings come to about 540 calories with around 60 grams of protein.

Which Wingstop flavour is lowest in sodium?

Plain, by a wide margin, at around 30 mg per wing. Cajun is around 310 mg for the same calories and the same protein. Garlic Parmesan at roughly 75 mg and Mango Habanero at about 80 mg are the next best. Across a ten wing order that choice is worth nearly 3,000 mg.

Are boneless wings better than classic at Wingstop?

No. Boneless wings are breaded chunks of breast meat. A classic bone-in wing carries roughly twice the protein of a boneless one, with less sodium and almost no carbohydrate. Order classic.

Is Wingstop good for weight loss?

The wings themselves are among the better protein sources in fast food at about 10 grams for 90 calories. What breaks it is the flavour choice, the ranch, and the fries. Six to ten plain or dry-rub classic wings with the dip on the side is a reasonable dinner.

How many calories are in 10 Wingstop wings?

Roughly 900 to 1,200 depending on the flavour, with around 100 grams of protein in a plain classic order. Add ranch and a lemon pepper flavour and the same order can pass 1,500.
