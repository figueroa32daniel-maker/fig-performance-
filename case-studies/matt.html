Where Matt started

Matt had lifted in his twenties and knew what he was doing. Between work and getting his kids where they needed to be, training had become something he squeezed in when a gap appeared, which is another way of saying it had become optional.

He tried a thirty-day challenge. He tried strict meal prep. Both worked exactly as long as his schedule held still, which was never very long.

What changed

He picked two studio sessions he could usually make, and kept a shorter workout for the weeks that fell apart. Friday pizza stayed where it was.

The moment it showed up

We ask every client the same question eventually, because the answer is never the one on the scale. It is the first ordinary thing that gets easier without being planned.

For Matt: Noticing that a missed Tuesday no longer turned into a missed week.

Why it held

Nothing here was dramatic. No thirty-day push, no plan that needed a quiet month to survive. A program you can run on a bad week beats a better program you abandon in March, and that is most of the difference between the 7 months that worked and the years before them that did not.

Can I train with Fig Performance if I travel for work?

Yes. Online coaching is built for it: a plan you follow at home plus a shorter version for hotel gyms, with a weekly video call. Several clients train this way.

No. Every program starts from an InBody scan and a movement assessment at the free consultation, and the first weights come from what you can do that day.

Yes. Fig Performance is a private studio and sessions are one on one with a coach. Semi-private runs in groups of three at most.
