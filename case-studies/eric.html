Where Eric started

Eric ran several days a week and lifted when it fit. He wanted a stronger upper body and he wanted to feel better on weekend hikes.

He added sets. He swapped exercises based on videos. He followed a six-day lifting split. Every one of them worked until a work trip broke the rhythm, and then he was starting over.

What changed

With online coaching he followed a shorter plan and logged the same key lifts week after week, so the plan stopped depending on a perfect month.

The moment it showed up

We ask every client the same question eventually, because the answer is never the one on the scale. It is the first ordinary thing that gets easier without being planned.

For Eric: Watching his rows and presses climb on the log, and knowing exactly where to pick up when he got home from a trip.

Why it held

Nothing here was dramatic. No thirty-day push, no plan that needed a quiet month to survive. A program you can run on a bad week beats a better program you abandon in March, and that is most of the difference between the 8 months that worked and the years before them that did not.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Can I train with Fig Performance if I travel for work?

Yes. Online coaching is built for it: a plan you follow at home plus a shorter version for hotel gyms, with a weekly video call. Several clients train this way.

Do I need to be fit before I start?

No. Every program starts from an InBody scan and a movement assessment at the free consultation, and the first weights come from what you can do that day.

Is training one on one?

Yes. Fig Performance is a private studio and sessions are one on one with a coach. Semi-private runs in groups of three at most.
