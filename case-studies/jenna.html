Where Jenna started

Jenna used her gym membership, but every visit looked the same. Treadmill, a few familiar machines, leave. She had workouts saved on her phone and never opened them.

The barrier was not motivation. It was standing in a busy weight room trying to work out a lift she had never done while people waited for the equipment.

What changed

At the studio she learned how to set a lift up, how to pick a starting weight, and how to tell when she was ready to add to it. That is a skill, and it travels.

The moment it showed up

We ask every client the same question eventually, because the answer is never the one on the scale. It is the first ordinary thing that gets easier without being planned.

For Jenna: Her first solo gym day with the new routine, when she walked past the treadmill and straight to the weights.

Why it held

Nothing here was dramatic. No thirty-day push, no plan that needed a quiet month to survive. A program you can run on a bad week beats a better program you abandon in March, and that is most of the difference between the 6 months that worked and the years before them that did not.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Can I train with Fig Performance if I travel for work?

Yes. Online coaching is built for it: a plan you follow at home plus a shorter version for hotel gyms, with a weekly video call. Several clients train this way.

Do I need to be fit before I start?

No. Every program starts from an InBody scan and a movement assessment at the free consultation, and the first weights come from what you can do that day.

Is training one on one?

Yes. Fig Performance is a private studio and sessions are one on one with a coach. Semi-private runs in groups of three at most.
