FAQ

Straight answers.

Who are these guides for?

Adults over 40, especially busy professionals, who already exercise and eat reasonably well but aren't seeing the results they want.

Who writes the guides?

Coach Dan Figueroa, ACSM certified personal trainer and owner of Fig Performance, a private one-on-one strength gym in Placentia, CA.

Is this medical advice?

No. These guides are general fitness information. Check with your physician before starting a new program, especially if you have a medical condition or injury.
