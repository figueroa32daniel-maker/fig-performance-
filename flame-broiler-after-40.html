Hot take: Flame Broiler is the only place on this list where the salt is not a problem.

About 340 mg of sodium in a full bowl. Del Taco's Epic burrito is 3,130.

Every other guide on this site has a paragraph warning you about sodium. Jersey Mike's can reach 3,530 mg in one sandwich. A Del Taco Epic burrito hits 3,130. A Panera bread bowl is around 2,170. A full Flame Broiler chicken bowl with white rice is about 340 mg. That is not a typo, and it is the reason this restaurant is worth knowing about.

The bowl, with the rice in it

  • Chicken Bowl, white rice: about 600 calories, roughly 45 g protein, about 14 g fat, around 340 mg sodium
  • Chicken and Beef Bowl, white rice: about 610 calories, roughly 48 g protein, around 440 mg sodium

Forty-five grams of protein in a full meal for 600 calories is already good. Getting it for 340 mg of sodium is the part nowhere else on this list can match.

The sauce is the whole sodium story

Look at the protein portion on its own, before rice:

  • Chicken, no sauce: about 270 calories, around 190 mg sodium
  • Chicken, original sauce: about 250 calories, roughly 39 g protein, around 580 mg sodium
  • White meat chicken, original sauce: about 240 calories, roughly 44 g protein, around 630 mg sodium

The sauce roughly triples the salt and adds almost nothing else. Same move as WaBa: ask for it on the side and you decide. The difference here is you are starting from a much better number, so even sauced this place beats most of the menu board in Placentia.

Three hundred and forty milligrams. The next closest full meal on this site is nearly three times that.

White meat, again

Same rule as WaBa Grill. White meat chicken comes in around 240 calories with roughly 44 grams of protein against about 250 and 39 for the regular chicken. Fewer calories, five more grams, less fat. Ask for it.

If you want the smaller version, a chicken mini bowl portion is about 160 calories with roughly 26 grams of protein and only around 115 mg of sodium.

What I would order

  1. White meat chicken bowl, sauce on the side. Around 45 grams of protein, and you control the salt.
  2. Half the rice if you are eating at a desk afterwards. The rice is most of the 600.
  3. Mini bowl when you want protein without a full meal. About 160 calories and 26 grams.
  4. Skip nothing else. There is no trap item here, which is rare.

The 15-second version

If you remember one thing

  • About 340 mg sodium in a full bowl, lowest on this site
  • Sauce roughly triples the salt, ask for it on the side
  • White meat: five more grams for fewer calories

Why this one is on the list

Because if a doctor has told you to watch your blood pressure, almost every other restaurant in these guides needs a caveat and this one does not. Grilled protein, rice, steamed vegetables, no fryer anywhere in the building, and a salt number that looks like something you cooked at home.

It is not exciting food. That is the point. The clients who keep their weight off have two or three boring, reliable orders they can make without thinking, and this is one of the best ones available in North Orange County.

A note on these numbers

One warning for anyone checking this against a nutrition app. Several databases list Flame Broiler "Regular Bowls, Single Protein" at 250 to 270 calories, and that is the protein portion on its own, not a bowl with rice. Reading those as a full meal would understate it badly. The 600 calorie figure above is the full chicken bowl with white rice, which two independent sources give identically at 45 g protein and 340 mg sodium. Portions at a bowl restaurant vary with whoever is serving, so treat all of this as close rather than exact, and ask your store for their current sheet if you are tracking tightly.

FAQ

Straight answers.

How much sodium is in a Flame Broiler bowl?

A full chicken bowl with white rice is around 340 mg, which is the lowest of any full meal in these restaurant guides. The sauce is where the salt concentrates, so asking for it on the side keeps it lower still.

How much protein is in a Flame Broiler chicken bowl?

About 45 grams in a full chicken bowl with white rice, at roughly 600 calories. A chicken and beef bowl is around 610 calories with about 48 grams.

Is Flame Broiler healthy?

It is one of the better options in North Orange County. Grilled protein, rice and steamed vegetables with no fryer in the building, around 45 grams of protein per bowl, and a sodium figure far below every other chain in these guides. The rice is most of the calories, so ask for half if you want it lighter.

Should I get white meat chicken at Flame Broiler?

Yes. White meat comes in around 240 calories with roughly 44 grams of protein, against about 250 calories and 39 grams for the regular chicken. Fewer calories, more protein, less fat.

Why do nutrition apps show Flame Broiler bowls at only 250 calories?

Because those entries are the protein portion on its own, not a bowl with rice. A full chicken bowl with white rice is closer to 600 calories. If you log the 250 figure for a full meal you will undercount by more than half.
