There are two words at the WaBa Grill counter that are worth about eight grams of protein, and they cost nothing. Ask for white meat. The White Meat Chicken Bowl comes to roughly 630 calories with about 46 grams of protein. The regular Chicken Bowl is about 640 calories with 38 grams. Same bowl, same price range, ten fewer calories, eight more grams of protein.

White meat against regular chicken

This is the only thing you need to remember about this restaurant:

  • White Meat Chicken Bowl: about 630 calories, roughly 46 g protein, about 5 g fat, around 880 mg sodium
  • Regular Chicken Bowl: about 640 calories, roughly 38 g protein, about 11 g fat

Eight grams of protein is not a rounding error at our age. It is the difference between a lunch that holds you to dinner and one that has you looking in the pantry at four o'clock. And unlike almost every other upgrade in fast food, this one does not cost you calories.

The veggie bowl is the better version of the same idea

Swapping some of the rice for steamed vegetables is the other free move here. A White Meat Chicken Veggie Bowl lands near 580 calories with roughly 47 grams of protein, which is a better number than the standard bowl in both directions.

The bowls at WaBa are carb-heavy by design. That is not automatically a problem, but if you are eating one at lunch and sitting at a desk afterwards, the veggie version is the one I would pick.

Two words at the counter. Eight grams of protein. Nobody tells you this.

The best protein-to-calorie item in this whole series

The Signature House Salad with white meat chicken is about 320 calories with roughly 44 grams of protein.

Stop and look at that. Forty-four grams of protein for 320 calories, at a drive-through, in North Orange County, for under ten dollars. Nothing at In-N-Out, Chick-fil-A, Chipotle or KFC touches that ratio. If you are trying to hit a protein target without spending calories, this is the single best order in any of these eleven guides.

A few others worth knowing, all white meat:

  • White Meat Chicken Mini Bowl: about 320 calories, roughly 21 g protein, around 630 mg sodium
  • Side of white meat chicken on its own: about 260 calories, roughly 40 g protein
  • White Meat Chicken Plate: about 800 calories, roughly 65 g protein, around 1,120 mg sodium

The sodium, and the wrong number going around

Current figures put a White Meat Chicken Bowl around 880 mg of sodium and the White Meat Chicken Veggie Bowl around 930 mg. For a full meal that is reasonable, better than a Jersey Mike's regular sub and far better than a Panera bread bowl.

You may run into a much scarier number online. Several sites report 2,455 mg for a WaBa chicken bowl. That figure traces back to a page last updated in July 2021, and it does not match anything current. If you have been avoiding this place because of a sodium number you read somewhere, it was probably that one, and it is five years out of date.

The one thing that is true: the WaBa sauce is soy based and it goes on everything, so it is where the salt is. Ask for it on the side and you control the amount.

Forty-four grams of protein for 320 calories. Nothing else in these eleven guides comes close.

Coach Dan Figueroa

What I would order

  1. Signature House Salad with white meat chicken. About 320 calories and roughly 44 grams of protein. Best protein-to-calorie order in any of these guides.
  2. White Meat Chicken Veggie Bowl if you want the rice. Around 580 calories and roughly 47 grams of protein.
  3. Say white meat every time. Eight grams of protein for nothing is the single best-value sentence in this series.
  4. Sauce on the side. It is where the sugar and most of the sodium live, and on the side you decide how much.
  5. Half the rice, more vegetables if you are eating this at lunch on a desk day.

Why this one is on the list

Because WaBa is on half the corners in this part of Orange County and almost nothing honest has been written about it. The national chains have armies of dietitians producing content. The regional ones that people here actually eat at every week get ignored, which is exactly why the one useful fact about this menu is not common knowledge.

It is a good restaurant for an adult over 40. Grilled protein, steamed vegetables, no fryer. It just has a default setting that quietly gives you less protein than the version standing next to it.

A note on these numbers

Published data for WaBa Grill is messier than for the national chains, so everything here is given as approximate. The white meat comparison and the 880 mg sodium figure each come from three independently maintained sources that agree, including one updated in November 2026. Figures that did not survive checking are named rather than quietly dropped: a widely syndicated 2,455 mg sodium figure traces to a page last updated in July 2021, a 120 mg figure on two sites is an obvious data entry error, and one database reports 60 g of protein in a chicken bowl, which no other source supports. Portions at a bowl restaurant vary with whoever is holding the scoop. Ask your store for their current sheet if you are tracking closely.
