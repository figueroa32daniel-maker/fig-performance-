Who we help in Yorba Linda

Yorba Linda calls itself the Land of Gracious Living, and the people Fig Performance is built for have earned it: business owners, executives and professionals in their 40s, 50s and 60s with full calendars and a long history of working out. The pattern we hear most from clients is "I'm doing everything right and nothing is changing." Usually something is changing, just not the number on the scale. We help them:

  • Find out what's actually happening. An InBody scan separates fat from muscle, so you stop guessing whether the last six months worked.
  • Lift heavier than "light weights." Muscle after 40 responds to progressive strength work, not endless cardio. Here's how that looks for women over 50, and for men over 40.
  • Protect what you love doing. Golf, tennis, hiking the Chino Hills trails, keeping up with grandkids. Training is built around the back, hips and shoulders those hobbies lean on.

Why Yorba Linda clients drive to Placentia

There are plenty of gyms closer to home. People choose Fig Performance for three reasons: every session is private and one-on-one, so there's no waiting on a rack or being watched; progress is measured with an InBody scan instead of a mirror and a hope; and the whole thing fits a working schedule, with the doors open at 5 AM on weekdays and Saturdays.

What training looks like

  1. Free consult and InBody scan. About an hour: body fat, muscle and visceral fat, a movement and strength assessment, and your goals.
  2. Your plan. Two or more 50-minute sessions a week, one-on-one with Coach Dan, plus a simple nutrition plan built from foods you already eat.
  3. Recover. Members use red light therapy and vibration therapy as part of the routine.
  4. Re-scan. The InBody shows what changed. Then we adjust. Scan. Plan. Train. Recover. Re-scan.

Coach Dan Figueroa is an ACSM certified personal trainer and a Precision Nutrition Level 1 certified nutrition coach. Every session is with him. No hand-offs to a junior trainer.
