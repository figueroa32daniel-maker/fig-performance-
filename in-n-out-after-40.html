Fitness after 40

Unpopular opinion: In-N-Out is not what is keeping you heavy, and I have never told a client to stop going.

Two items on that board are doing almost all of the damage. The burger is not one of them.

Most trainers would tell you to stay out of In-N-Out. I have never once told a client to stop going. A plan that bans the place your family already eats at is a plan you quit by March, and in this case the ban would be aimed at the wrong thing anyway. In-N-Out is one of the easier places in Orange County to eat well. Two items on that board are doing almost all of the damage, and the burger is not one of them.

Key takeaways

  • A Double-Double gives you 34 g of protein for about 610 calories. A regular fries gives you 6 g for 360.
  • Protein Style saves about 150 calories and 30 g of carbs on any burger, and the protein does not change.
  • The order: Double-Double Protein Style, mustard and ketchup instead of spread.

The numbers that matter

In-N-Out publishes its nutrition information, which is more than a lot of chains do. Here is the short version for the items people actually order.

  • Hamburger: about 360 calories
  • Cheeseburger: about 480 calories
  • Double-Double: about 610 calories, 34 g protein
  • Fries, regular: about 360 calories, 6 g protein

Look at the last two lines together, because that is the whole article. A Double-Double gives you 34 grams of protein for 610 calories. A regular order of fries gives you 6 grams for 360. One of those is food. The other is a side that costs you most of a meal and leaves you hungry an hour later.

The burger is not your problem. The fries and the shake are your problem.

Coach Dan Figueroa

Protein Style, and what it actually saves

Ordering Protein Style swaps the bun for a lettuce wrap. The crew does not blink; roughly everyone orders it at some point.

It takes about 150 calories and close to 30 grams of carbohydrate off any burger on the menu, and the protein does not move. A Double-Double goes from about 610 calories to about 460, and still carries around 30 grams of protein.

Whether that is worth it depends on you. If you are over 40, training twice a week and trying to hold muscle while losing fat, 30 grams of protein for 460 calories is a genuinely good meal. If you are not in a deficit and you like the bun, eat the bun. The lettuce wrap is a tool, not a virtue.

What I would order

  1. Double-Double, Protein Style, no spread, mustard and ketchup instead. About 460 calories before the swap, and the sauce change takes off another 50 to 60. Over 30 grams of protein. This is the order.
  2. Cheeseburger Protein Style if you want something lighter. Roughly 280 to 330 calories and around 16 to 19 grams of protein.
  3. Skip the fries, or share one order. Not because fries are evil, but because 360 calories buys you almost no protein and no fullness. If you want them, they are the thing to spend on, not an automatic add.
  4. Water or a diet drink. A shake is the single biggest number on the board and it is liquid, which means it does nothing for hunger.

The 15-second version

If you remember one thing

  • Double-Double Protein Style, mustard and ketchup
  • Fries are a choice, not a default
  • Drink something with no calories

Why I do not tell people to avoid it

Because they go anyway, and then they feel like they blew it, and then the rest of the day goes with it. That cycle costs far more than a burger.

What actually moves the needle for the adults I coach is being able to eat at the places their family already goes and still land near their protein target. In-N-Out is one of the easiest places in Orange County to do that. Chipotle and Chick-fil-A are the other two.

If you want the broader version of this, I wrote a guide to high-protein fast food after 40, and a free restaurant playbook you can keep on your phone. If protein is the thing you keep missing, start with how much you actually need.

One note on the numbers

Nutrition aggregator sites disagree about In-N-Out by 20 to 60 calories on several items, usually because they are guessing at the spread or at a Protein Style build. I have used In-N-Out's own published figures here and rounded. Check the current chart in store or on their site if you are tracking closely, because menus and recipes change and this page will not update itself.

The rest of the restaurant guides

  • Chipotle: 81 g of protein, or 1,400 calories
  • Chick-fil-A: grilled nuggets, 25 g for 130 calories
  • El Pollo Loco: 34 g in a grilled breast, and the sodium
  • Starbucks: protein lattes and egg bites
  • Raising Cane's: the sauce costs more than the chicken
  • Jersey Mike's: the three words that cut 300 calories
  • Panera: the bread bowl holds more than the soup
  • KFC: Extra Crispy costs 140 calories for nothing
  • Del Taco: three street tacos beat the salad
  • WaBa Grill: say white meat, eight more grams free
  • Flame Broiler: about 340 mg of sodium in a full bowl, the lowest on this site
  • Five Guys: a bunless burger is 440 calories, 32 g protein and almost no salt
  • Jack in the Box: grilled strips are about 200 calories for 30 g of protein

All of them follow the same idea: find the protein, spot the one item doing the damage, and order so you are not the person at the table eating differently from everyone else.

FAQ

Straight answers.

What is the healthiest thing to order at In-N-Out?

A Double-Double Protein Style with mustard and ketchup instead of spread. It comes in around 400 to 460 calories with over 30 grams of protein, which is a solid meal for anyone trying to hold muscle while losing fat.

How many calories does Protein Style save?

About 150 calories and close to 30 grams of carbohydrate on any burger on the menu, because the bun is doing almost all of that work. The protein stays the same.

How much protein is in a Double-Double?

About 34 grams in the standard version and about 30 grams Protein Style. That is a full meal's worth of protein for most adults.

Are In-N-Out fries bad for you?

They are not bad for you, they are just a poor trade. A regular order is about 360 calories and gives you 6 grams of protein, so it costs most of a meal and does very little for fullness. Share one, or spend those calories on the burger.

Can I eat fast food and still lose fat after 40?

Yes, if the meals you build hit your protein target and fit your calories. The clients who do best are not the ones who avoid restaurants, they are the ones who have three or four orders they can make anywhere without thinking about it.
