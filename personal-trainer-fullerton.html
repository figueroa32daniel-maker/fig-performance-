Who we help in Fullerton

Fullerton is a university town with a major hospital and a busy downtown, so if you work on campus, on a hospital schedule or in an office, you know what it's like to run out of day before you get to the gym. The same goes for anyone who has belonged to the same Fullerton gym for years and quietly stopped seeing changes. Many of our clients are over 50. Some are over 70. We help them:

  • Build muscle at any age. Teresa, 60, and Sam, 70, started from almost nothing. Sam now deadlifts 135 pounds. Teresa did her first pull-up in about three months.
  • Train around long shifts. We're open 5 AM to 8 PM on weekdays and 5 AM to noon on Saturdays, so sessions fit around hospital, campus and clinic schedules.
  • Lose fat without losing muscle. The InBody scan tracks both, which matters most for anyone over 40, and especially for anyone on a GLP-1 medication. Here's how we handle that.

Why Fullerton clients drive to Placentia

Fullerton has no shortage of gyms, and most of them are busy at the hours a working adult can train. Fig Performance is the opposite: a private floor, one coach, one client. There's no waiting for equipment, no crowd, and no guesswork about whether you're getting stronger, because every lift is tracked and every few months the InBody shows what changed.

What training looks like

  1. Free consult and InBody scan. About an hour: body fat, muscle and visceral fat, a movement and strength assessment, and your goals.
  2. Your plan. Two or more 50-minute sessions a week, one-on-one with Coach Dan, plus a simple nutrition plan built from foods you already eat.
  3. Recover. Members use red light therapy and vibration therapy as part of the routine.
  4. Re-scan. The InBody shows what changed. Then we adjust. Scan. Plan. Train. Recover. Re-scan.

Coach Dan Figueroa is an ACSM certified personal trainer and a Precision Nutrition Level 1 certified nutrition coach. Every session is with him. No hand-offs to a junior trainer.

Pricing

Personal training is $90 a session, billed weekly or monthly, with a 3-month minimum. It's $72 a session when you pay for 3 months upfront. Semi-private small-group training is $199 to $345 a month, and an open-gym membership is $79.99 a month. See plans and pricing or the personal training cost guide.
